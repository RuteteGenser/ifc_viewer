Argus uses only cookies that are strictly necessary for the service to work. That is why we do not ask for consent and you see no consent banner. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and none from third parties.

Local storage in the browser

The app also stores some data locally on your device: cases and notes, settings, and a cache so the app works offline. This is not sent to us. You can delete it by clearing the site data for Argus in your browser. That also removes cases you have not exported.

Payment

When you pay, you are sent to Stripe or Vipps MobilePay. Their own cookie rules apply on their pages.
