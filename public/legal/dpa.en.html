3. Instructions

The Supplier processes the data only on documented instructions from the Customer. This agreement, the Terms of Service and the Customer's use of the service's functions (save, move, delete, download) are the Customer's instructions. The Supplier tells the Customer at once if, in its opinion, an instruction breaks data protection law.

4. Confidentiality

Everyone who processes the data for the Supplier is bound by confidentiality. The Supplier does not open the contents of models unless the Customer asks for help or the law requires it.

5. Security

The Supplier has measures under GDPR Art. 32 appropriate to the risk, including encrypted transfer (HTTPS), access control, passwords stored as a secure hash, backups, system updates and logging of important events. The Supplier reviews the measures regularly.

7. Transfers outside the EEA

Data is not transferred outside the EEA without a valid basis under GDPR Chapter V, for example the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

8. Assistance and breaches

  • The Supplier helps the Customer, as far as possible, to respond to requests from data subjects (access, rectification, erasure and similar), with security, data protection impact assessments (DPIA) and prior consultation with the Data Protection Authority.
  • The Supplier notifies the Customer without undue delay, and at the latest within 48 hours after becoming aware of it, of any personal data breach affecting the Customer's data. The notice describes what is known about the breach, its consequences and the measures taken.

9. When the agreement ends

When the Customer stops using the service, the Customer can download its models (see section 8 of the Terms of Service). The Supplier then deletes the data, also from backups within [NUMBER OF BACKUP DAYS], unless the law requires it to be kept. The Supplier confirms the deletion in writing if the Customer asks.

10. Audits

The Supplier gives the Customer the information needed to show that this agreement is followed. The Customer may carry out an audit, itself or through an independent auditor bound by confidentiality, with reasonable notice (at least 30 days) and at most once a year, unless a breach gives reason for more. The Customer covers its own costs.
