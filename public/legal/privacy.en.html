4. How long we keep data

  • Account: until you delete it. When you delete your account, the account and your models are deleted from our system at once. Backups are overwritten within [NUMBER OF BACKUP DAYS].
  • Trial without an account: deleted after 180 days without use if you have not saved any models.
  • Saved models after access has ended: you can download them during the read-only period and for 30 days after. Then they are deleted. We warn you at least 7 days before.
  • Payments and receipts: as long as the Bookkeeping Act requires, normally 5 years after the end of the financial year.
  • Technical data from payment providers (webhooks): the content is removed after 90 days. Only the ID and type are kept.
  • Acceptance of terms: as long as the account exists, and then as long as we need it to document the contract.
  • Web server logs: [LOGGLAGRING ANTALL DAGER] days.

6. Transfers outside the EEA

We try to keep data in the EEA. If a provider transfers data to a country outside the EEA (for example Stripe to the USA), this only happens with a valid basis under GDPR Chapter V: an adequacy decision of the European Commission (for the USA: the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for certified companies) or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) with the necessary supplementary measures. You can ask us for a copy of the basis.

8. Security

  • All traffic is encrypted over HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored only as a secure hash. We cannot see your password.
  • IP addresses are stored by us only as a keyed hash.
  • Cookies are marked HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite.
  • Access to servers and data is limited to those who need it.
  • We take backups and keep systems updated.
  • Card data is handled only by Stripe, and Vipps data by Vipps MobilePay.

If a security breach is likely to pose a high risk to you, we tell you without undue delay, and we notify the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet) when the law requires it.

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you,
  • rectification of incorrect data,
  • erasure of data (you can delete your account yourself under Account),
  • data portability, that is, get your data in a machine-readable format (under Account you find "Download my account data" and "Download all my models"),
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest,
  • restriction of processing.

Email [E-POST]. We answer within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm who you are.

You can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority if you think we process data against the rules: datatilsynet.no, Postboks 458 Sentrum, 0105 Oslo, Norway.

10. Children

You must be at least 13 years old to use Argus. You must be at least 18 to buy a subscription. We do not knowingly collect data about children under 13. If we learn that we have such data, we delete it.

11. Changes

We update this policy when we change how we process data. For material changes we tell you in the app or by email before the change applies.
