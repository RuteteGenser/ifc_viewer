What you buy

A subscription to Argus, a browser viewer for IFC files (building models, BIM). With the subscription you can:

  • open and view IFC models in the browser, measure and section them,
  • save models in "My models" and open them from all your devices, within the storage limit of the plan,
  • use the app on phone, tablet and computer.

The service is delivered digitally and starts as soon as the payment is approved. It works in current versions of common browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
