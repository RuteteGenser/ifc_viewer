2. What the service is

Argus is a browser viewer for IFC files (building models, BIM). You can:

  • open IFC files in the browser and view, measure and section the model,
  • save models in "My models" on our servers, so you can open them from other devices,
  • create cases and notes (Cases). They are stored only locally on your device.

Files you open stay on your device. They are only uploaded to us if you save them to "My models" yourself.

3. Account

You can try the service without an account. To buy a subscription you need an account with an email address and a password.

  • Use an email address you have access to.
  • Keep your password secret. Tell us at once if you think someone else has used your account.
  • You must be at least 13 years old to use the service, and at least 18 to buy a subscription.
  • You can delete your account at any time under Account. This deletes the account and the models you have saved with us. Payment records are kept as long as the Norwegian Bookkeeping Act requires.

4. Free trial and access codes

  • The first time you open the app on a device, you get a free trial for 24 hours. It needs no account and no payment, and it does not turn into a paid subscription by itself.
  • An access code gives access for the period stated on the code. A code is personal unless we say otherwise, and cannot be exchanged for money.
  • We may limit how many trials are started from the same network, to prevent abuse.

5. Subscription, prices and VAT

  • We offer monthly and yearly subscriptions. The current price and what the plan includes (for example how much you can store) are shown before you pay.

  • The subscription starts when the payment is approved.
  • Each plan has storage limits (number of models and total size). When you reach a limit, you can delete models or choose a larger plan. We do not delete anything because you are at a limit.

  • How to cancel: Go to Account and tap Cancel subscription. It is as easy as buying. If you paid with Vipps, you can also stop the agreement in the Vipps app. You can also email [E-POST].

  • For yearly subscriptions we send you an email reminder at least 30 days before renewal.

  • If you owe us money for a period you have used, we may collect it under the normal rules.

9. Right of withdrawal for consumers

If you are a consumer, you have a 14-day right of withdrawal under the Norwegian Cancellation Act (angrerettloven). The period starts the day after the contract is made, that is the day after your first purchase.

How to withdraw: Tell us within the period, for example by email to [E-POST]. You can use the withdrawal form, but you do not have to. A clear statement that you want to withdraw is enough.

Delivery during the withdrawal period: Argus is a service that starts at once. When you buy, we therefore ask you to tick a box to request that the service starts right away. The text is:

I ask for the service to start right away. I understand that my right of withdrawal ends when the service has been fully delivered, and that if I withdraw before then, I pay for the part I have used.

This means:

  • If you withdraw within 14 days, you only pay for the time from the start of the subscription until you told us. You get the rest back. Example: if you withdraw after 3 days of a 30-day month, you pay 3/30 of the monthly price.
  • Once the service has been fully delivered (the period you paid for is used up), you no longer have a right of withdrawal for that period.
  • [CONFIRM: voluntary policy] On top of this, we voluntarily refund your whole first payment if you withdraw within 14 days of the first payment. This does not apply to renewals.

We refund at the latest 14 days after we received your notice, using the same payment method you used, free of charge.

Businesses have no right of withdrawal.
