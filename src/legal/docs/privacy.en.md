This explains what personal data we process when you use {{facts.product}}, why, and what rights you have under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Norwegian Personal Data Act (personopplysningsloven).

## 1. Controller

{{seller.name}}, organisation number {{seller.orgNr}}, {{seller.address}}, is the controller. Send privacy questions to {{seller.email}}.

## 2. The short version

- IFC files you open stay on your device. They are not sent to us unless you save them to "My models" yourself.
- Cases and notes are stored only on your device.
- We use only strictly necessary cookies. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking across websites.
- We never sell data about you.

| Data | Purpose | Legal basis |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Which version of the terms you accepted, when, and your request for delivery during the withdrawal period | Document the contract and the withdrawal choice | Legal obligation and legitimate interest in documenting the contract (Art. 6(1)(c) and (f)) |
| IP address (stored by us only as a keyed hash), short browser type, technical logs | Security, preventing abuse and attacks, troubleshooting | Legitimate interest in a secure and stable service (Art. 6(1)(f)) |

| Messages you send us | Answer you | Contract or legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f)) |

We do not send marketing without your consent.

Building models can contain personal data, for example names of people in a project. For business customers, the customer is the controller of such data in the models, and we are the processor. See the [data processing agreement](doc:dpa).

## 4. How long we keep data

- **Account:** until you delete it. When you delete your account, the account and your models are deleted from our system at once. Backups are overwritten within [NUMBER OF BACKUP DAYS].
- **Trial without an account:** deleted after {{facts.anonInactiveDays}} days without use if you have not saved any models.
- **Saved models after access has ended:** you can download them during the read-only period and for {{facts.retentionDays}} days after. Then they are deleted. We warn you at least {{facts.purgeNoticeDays}} days before.
- **Payments and receipts:** as long as the Bookkeeping Act requires, normally 5 years after the end of the financial year.
- **Technical data from payment providers (webhooks):** the content is removed after {{facts.paymentPayloadDays}} days. Only the ID and type are kept.
- **Acceptance of terms:** as long as the account exists, and then as long as we need it to document the contract.
- **Web server logs:** {{seller.logDays}} days.

## 6. Transfers outside the EEA

We try to keep data in the EEA. If a provider transfers data to a country outside the EEA (for example Stripe to the USA), this only happens with a valid basis under GDPR Chapter V: an adequacy decision of the European Commission (for the USA: the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for certified companies) or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) with the necessary supplementary measures. You can ask us for a copy of the basis.

## 7. Cookies

We only use cookies that are strictly necessary for the service to work. Such cookies do not need consent under the Norwegian Electronic Communications Act (ekomloven). We use no analytics or marketing cookies. See the [cookie list](doc:cookies).

| Name | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `argus_dev` | Recognises your device, so the trial and models saved without an account follow the device | {{facts.deviceCookieDays}} days |
| `argus_sid` | Keeps you signed in | {{facts.sessionCookieDays}} days, or until you sign out |

The app also stores data locally in your browser (for example cases, notes and settings, and a cache so the app works offline). It stays on your device and is not sent to us.

## 8. Security

- All traffic is encrypted over HTTPS.
- Passwords are stored only as a secure hash. We cannot see your password.
- IP addresses are stored by us only as a keyed hash.
- Cookies are marked HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite.
- Access to servers and data is limited to those who need it.
- We take backups and keep systems updated.
- Card data is handled only by Stripe, and Vipps data by Vipps MobilePay.

If a security breach is likely to pose a high risk to you, we tell you without undue delay, and we notify the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet) when the law requires it.

## 9. Your rights

You have the right to:

- **access** the data we hold about you,
- **rectification** of incorrect data,
- **erasure** of data (you can delete your account yourself under Account),
- **data portability**, that is, get your data in a machine-readable format (under Account you find "Download my account data" and "Download all my models"),
- **object** to processing based on legitimate interest,
- **restriction** of processing.

Email {{seller.email}}. We answer within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm who you are.

You can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority if you think we process data against the rules: [datatilsynet.no](https://www.datatilsynet.no), Postboks 458 Sentrum, 0105 Oslo, Norway.

## 10. Children

You must be at least 13 years old to use {{facts.product}}. You must be at least 18 to buy a subscription. We do not knowingly collect data about children under 13. If we learn that we have such data, we delete it.

## 11. Changes

We update this policy when we change how we process data. For material changes we tell you in the app or by email before the change applies.
