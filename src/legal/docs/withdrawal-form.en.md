# Withdrawal form

> **UTKAST / DRAFT: ikke juridisk vurdert.** Not legally reviewed. The standard model withdrawal form (Norwegian Cancellation Act and EU Consumer Rights Directive, Annex I B). Must be checked by a Norwegian lawyer before launch.

Attachment to the [Terms of Service](doc:terms#9-right-of-withdrawal-for-consumers).

You do not have to use this form. A clear message, for example by email, that you want to withdraw from the contract is enough. Send it before the 14-day withdrawal period ends.
